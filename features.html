Work Pulse Capabilities

Everything Built Into Work Pulse

A streamlined overview of task workflows, revision tracking, automation rules, and live team insights powering the workspace.

9 Core Modules Firebase Realtime Sync
Workflow

V1 Sent & Pending Feedback Stage

Separately tracks tasks in progress whose draft has already been shared for review or client sign-off.

  • Groups awaiting-feedback tasks under an amber V1 Sent sub-section inside In Progress.
  • Records sent version (V1, V2…), reviewer name, and live waiting days.
  • One-click Got Feedback · Resume returns the task to active work and bumps the next suggested version.
Workflow

48-Hour Focus & Collapsed Cards

Keeps the board scannable by surfacing immediate priorities and hiding distant or older items behind one toggle.

  • All cards start compact across To Do, In Progress, V1 Sent, and Completed.
  • Tasks due beyond 48 hours or completed over 48 hours ago fold under See more tasks.
  • Completed lane automatically orders tasks newest to oldest, while overdue items always stay pinned up top.
Workflow

Owners, 2nd Owners & External Names

Every task captures clear accountability across internal team members and outside collaborators.

  • Enforces mandatory Task Title and primary Owner on create, edit, and recurring forms.
  • Optional Secondary Owner (2nd) tag for shared execution.
  • Other (Type name…) option lets you assign tasks to external partners or clients and filter by them.
Logs

Follow-up Log & Mail Trail

Preserves complete communication context and follow-up cadence directly inside every expanded card.

  • Set follow-up dates, click ✓ Followed Up, and schedule next touchpoints (+1d, +3d, +1 Week).
  • Mark executive escalations with the VP checkbox for a corner red dot marker and dedicated quick filter.
  • Timestamped Follow-up Log entries and collapsible Mail Trail / Description drawers preserve full context.
Logs

Versioned Feedback Log

Automatically builds an audit trail of every version sent for approval and any feedback received.

  • Logs each round (V1, V2…) with reviewer, sent time, and status (Pending, Received, Approved).
  • Attach, edit, and copy revision summaries directly inside any round in Feedback Log.
  • History remains inspectable even after resuming work or moving the task to Completed.
Insights

14-Day Open Workload Heatmap

Visualizes upcoming deadline load across Current Week and Next Week without completed work skewing intensity.

  • Cell intensity reflects strictly open tasks, keeping active bottlenecks unmistakable.
  • Past and mixed days display completed task counts clearly in emerald green badges.
  • Click any day cell to instantly filter all board columns to that date.
Automation

Recurring Schedules Engine

Automates routine team deliverables cleanly across flexible cadences with zero manual re-entry.

  • Supports Daily, Weekdays, Weekly (day picker), Biweekly, Monthly, and Custom day intervals.
  • Choose between Auto-next on completion or filling the 14-day horizon.
  • Includes + Spawn Next, pause/resume, and full edit controls.
Automation

11:00 PM Deadline Auto-Rollover

Automatically carries missed open tasks forward into the next working day so nothing slips through.

  • Moves past-due open tasks after 11:00 PM while leaving completed work untouched (suppressed in View-Only).
  • Treats Sat & Sun as non-working days so missed Friday tasks roll directly over to Monday.
  • Mark fixed commitments with HD (Hard Deadline) to bypass auto-rollover and show directly as Overdue until completed.
  • Preserves original dates with → Moved ×N badges and Auto-Moved (11 PM) filtering.
Insights & Access

Completion Analytics & View-Only Mode

Measures per-owner execution speed and safeguards the workspace with two distinct passcode tiers.

  • Analytics (full-access only) calculates average days from creation to completion per owner with min–max & drilldowns.
  • Dedicated View-Only (0000) login hides edit controls, blocks writes, and restricts access to the Analytics screen.
  • Top bar displays live Total / In Progress / Done metrics synced across devices.
